01 / THE CHALLENGE

The problem isn't the cut. It's the setup.

Traditional setups often require shimming, blocking, and repeated repositioning. Every additional setup costs valuable production time and introduces another opportunity for error.

01

Too much repositioning

Multiple setups slow production and create opportunities for alignment issues.

02

Limited cutter access

Traditional workholding can restrict tool paths and limit accessible machining surfaces.

03

Lost production time

Time spent building complicated setups is time your spindle isn't making chips.

02 / THE SOLUTION

Elevate your workpiece. Open up possibilities.

The Libert Elevation System raises your workpiece off the machine table, creating clearance underneath and around the part. Improve cutter access, reduce repositioning, and spend more time making chips.

01

Modular by design

Configure the system around your workpiece instead of adapting every job to fixed workholding.

02

Faster setups

Reduce shimming, blocking, and repeated positioning before machining begins.

03

Greater cutter access

Create the clearance needed to machine more surfaces before repositioning your workpiece.
